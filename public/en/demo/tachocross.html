How it is calculated

Rules the panel uses for deadlines, discards and the two sources.

Legal download deadlines: driver card, every 28 days at most; vehicle unit (tachograph), every 90 days como máximo. En la columna "Legal deadline" se cuenta desde la descarga más reciente de VDO o Webfleet: verde en plazo, ámbar si quedan 7 días o menos y rojo si está vencido.

Always calculated from today's date. Days since the last download, the legal deadline and discards are recalculated every time you open the page. Se descarta a quien lleva 1 año o más sin descargar: today, a quien tiene su última descarga el or earlier.

Same rigour for every record. Even if a driver or vehicle appears in only one of the two sources, it is treated the same way: remote downloads from Webfleet are transferred to VDO. Even so, there may be manual downloads that arrive by email or other means and are uploaded by hand, so they can appear only in VDO.
